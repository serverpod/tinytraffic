# Game assets

Normalized copies of the assets picked for the game. Generated by `../pipeline/build.mjs`, so
change the pipeline and rebuild instead of editing these files. To add a new model, put the
original in `../Extras-glb/` (not here: rebuilding replaces everything in this folder except this
README) and add it to the list in `build.mjs`. `assets.json` lists every asset
with its size, grid footprint and source file.

## Conventions

- **Units:** 1 unit = 1 metre, +Y up.
- **Grid:** one road tile is **12 × 12 m**. Road tiles, sidewalk and grass tiles are centred on
  their tile. Tile `(x, z)` is centred at `(x * 12, 0, z * 12)`.
- **Pivot:** centre of the footprint, resting on `y = 0`. Trees pivot on the trunk.
- **Facing:** cars and buildings face **+Z** (car front, building entrance/storefront).
- **Heights:** the asphalt (11.1 m wide) is at `y = -0.03`, the rumble kerbs at `y = 0.02`; the sidewalk tile is a 0.15 m kerb above the road.
- **Lanes:** three 3.6 m lanes; their centres are 3.6 m apart, the middle one on the tile's centre line. Roads are one mesh with one material, coloured per vertex (`COLOR_0`).

## Assets

| Name | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| `road_straight` | Three lanes, runs along Z. Rotate 90° for east–west. Generated by `../pipeline/roads.mjs`. |
| `road_crosswalk` | Straight three-lane road with a zebra crossing. |
| `road_corner` | Connects east (+X) and south (+Z): a quarter ring around the tile corner at (+6, +6), paved outside the ring. Rotate in 90° steps for other turns. |
| `building_large_a`–`g`, `building_small_a`–`f` | City Kit, all scaled ×8 (≈3.4 m per storey). `footprint` in `assets.json` says how many tiles each one fills (1×1, 2×1 or 2×2). |

| `tree_a`–`c` | Nature pack trees, 6.1–7.8 m tall, simplified to 2.1k–2.7k triangles (bark mesh simplified, 40% of the leaf cards kept and enlarged). Leaves use alpha-test (`MASK`). |
| `pickup_coin` | Upright coin, 1.8 m across (just under a car's width), facing +Z, resting on `y = 0`, with a star on each face. Generated by `../pipeline/props.mjs`; light grey on material `Gold`, which the game tints gold. |
| `pickup_magnet`, `pickup_double` | Power-ups: a horseshoe magnet and a green-rimmed "x2" token, standing upright like the coin (about 1.9 m tall). Generated by `../pipeline/props.mjs`, coloured per vertex. |
| `vehicle_bus` | A 10 × 2.5 m city bus following the cars' node and material contract (`body` with `Paint`, `Window`, lamps; `wheel_front_left` etc. on their hubs, radius 0.5 m). |
| `obstacle_barrier`, `obstacle_cone` | Roadworks closing one lane: a striped barrier about 2.7 m across (its lamps on material `BlinkLamp`), and one big orange traffic cone, 2.2 m tall on a 1.7 m orange base, so it reads on a watch. |
| `ground_sidewalk`, `ground_grass` | Generated 12 m tiles (no source pack has sidewalks). |

## Using with flutter_scene

Checked against flutter_scene 0.24.3 (pure-Dart importer):

- The files use no glTF extensions: plain PNG/JPEG textures, 16-bit indices, metallic-roughness
  materials. Nothing needs a decoder.
- Convert them at build time with `buildScenes` in `hook/build.dart` (it picks up `.glb` files
  under the app's `assets/`), then `loadScene('assets/<name>.glb')`. `Node.fromGlbAsset` loads
  them at runtime without the hook.
- The build-time converter stores textures as raw RGBA8 unless `compressTextures: true` is passed,
  so turn that on. All texture sizes here are multiples of 4, as it requires.
- The converter ignores glTF samplers (textures always repeat). None of these assets rely on
  clamping.
- flutter_scene needs Flutter ≥ 3.47 and Flutter GPU enabled on native platforms
  (`--enable-flutter-gpu`, or the `FLTEnableFlutterGPU` Info.plist key / Android manifest flag).

## Licences

The game ships none of the pack models: its roads, sidewalk, coin, power-ups and obstacles are
generated here (`../pipeline/roads.mjs`, `props.mjs` and `build.mjs`), and its cars, buildings and
trees by the Blender scripts in `../cars`, `../buildings` and `../trees`. The pack copies in this
folder (City Pack cars, CC-BY, see `City Pack attribution.rtf`; City Kit buildings; Ultimate
Stylized Nature Pack trees, whose licences weren't included with the downloads) are reference only;
using any of them in the game again would need their credit and licences checked.
