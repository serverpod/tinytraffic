# Trees

Four low-poly trees in the matte toy style of `../cars`, with faceted, slightly irregular
canopies: `tree_oak`, `tree_maple`, `tree_maple_b`, `tree_poplar` (`.glb`). The maples are the
oak's lower, wider cousins: `tree_maple` has a forked trunk under a broad flat crown,
`tree_maple_b` a taller trunk splitting three ways under a lopsided dome. `review.jpg` shows
them next to the sedan, from a 3/4 view and from above.

They are generated by `build_trees.py` in Blender, so change the script and rebuild instead of
editing the GLBs:

```bash
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --factory-startup -P build_trees.py
```

Add tree names after `--` to build only those, and `--render DIR` to render the review views.

## Conventions

Same as `../game-assets`: 1 unit = 1 m, +Y up, the pivot is the centre of the trunk's base on
`y = 0`. No glTF extensions, no textures: positions, normals, 16-bit indices and
metallic-roughness materials.

| Tree | Height | Canopy radius | Triangles | File |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| oak | 6.80 m | 2.83 m | 596 | 19 KB |
| maple | 6.04 m | 3.02 m | 436 | 14 KB |
| maple_b | 6.35 m | 3.02 m | 546 | 17 KB |
| poplar | 7.45 m | 1.61 m | 232 | 9 KB |

Canopy radius is the furthest any part reaches from the trunk's axis.

## Nodes and materials

```
tree_<name>     root; glTF extras: height, canopyRadius, leavesMaterial ("Leaves"),
│               trunkMaterial ("Trunk")
└── tree        one mesh, one primitive per material
```

| Material | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `Leaves` | The whole canopy. Recolour it for seasons or variety. |
| `Trunk` | Trunk and branches. |

The canopies are flat-shaded where faces meet at more than 25°, and their vertices are nudged
by up to 7% (seeded per tree, so rebuilds are identical): `CANOPY_ANGLE` and `JITTER` at the
top of `build_trees.py` set how blocky they look.
