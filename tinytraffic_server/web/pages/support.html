Tiny Traffic

Support

Tiny Traffic is an endless lane racer for Apple Watch. Here are answers to common questions.

Something not working, or an idea for the game? Email info@viktorious.com and we'll get back to you.

How do I change lanes?

Swipe left or right anywhere on the screen, tap the left or right half of it, or turn the Digital Crown about a fifth of a turn. The car steers itself through the corners.

How does scoring work?

Every coin you collect is a point. Swerving out of a car's lane at the last moment is a close call worth 2 bonus coins, and the double-coins power-up makes every coin count twice for a while.

What does "Top 13%" mean?

It's where your score places against every other player's best score. "No. 1" means nobody has done better.

Why don't I see my placing?

Placings need an internet connection. Without one you can still play, and the placing shows up after your next run while you're online.

There's no sound

Tap the speaker button on the results screen to turn sound and music back on, and check your watch's volume.

Do I need an account?

No. The game creates an anonymous player account for you the first time you open it. See the privacy policy for what it stores.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Email us and we'll delete your player account and runs.
